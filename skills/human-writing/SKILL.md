# Human writing

People stop reading the moment a text smells generated. Write like a smart person explaining something to a colleague they respect: concrete, direct, a little personal, never padded.

## Before writing
Answer in one line each: who reads this, what they should know or do afterwards, and which tone fits (du/Sie, ты/вы, formal/casual). Default for reports and business texts: conversational-professional - clear, warm, no bureaucratese.

## Rules
1. **Lead with the point.** First sentence = the most important fact or the answer. No warm-up ("In today's fast-paced world…", "В современном мире…", "In der heutigen schnelllebigen Zeit…").
2. **Concrete over abstract.** Numbers, names, dates, examples, what actually happened. Replace every "significant improvement" with the actual number.
3. **Vary rhythm.** Mix short and long sentences. A three-word sentence is allowed. So is starting with "And" or "But" (И / Но / Und / Aber).
4. **Active voice, real subjects.** "Wir haben den Preis gesenkt", not "Es wurde eine Preissenkung vorgenommen". "Мы сократили сроки", not "Было осуществлено сокращение сроков".
5. **Cut filler.** No throat-clearing, no restating the question, no summary that repeats the text, no "It's important to note that", "Стоит отметить, что", "Es ist wichtig zu beachten, dass".
6. **Use structure only when it helps.** Bullets for real lists, tables for real comparisons. Not every paragraph needs a heading; not every idea comes in threes.
7. **Have a voice.** Opinions where appropriate, a light touch of humor if the context allows, the occasional rhetorical question. Admit uncertainty plainly ("Wir wissen noch nicht, ob…").
8. **Native language, not translation.** Write directly in German or Russian with that language's idioms and typography: German „…“ quotes, Russian «…» quotes, correct dashes, non-breaking spaces before units.

## AI tells to remove
See `references/ai-tells.md` for the full lists per language. The worst offenders:
- Words: delve, tapestry, landscape, realm, robust, seamless, leverage, elevate, unlock, crucial, pivotal, "in conclusion"; DE: „nahtlos“, „maßgeschneidert“, „ganzheitlich“, „im Folgenden“, „zusammenfassend lässt sich sagen“; RU: «является», «осуществлять», «данный», «в рамках», «играет ключевую роль», «в заключение стоит отметить».
- Patterns: "It's not just X, it's Y"; triplets everywhere ("fast, secure and scalable"); em-dash chains; colon headlines ("Revenue: A Deep Dive"); every paragraph ending with a moral; excessive bold; emoji bullets; ending with an offer ("Let me know if…") in documents.

## Editing pass (always do it)
1. Read the text as the target reader. Mark every sentence that could appear in any company's text - rewrite or delete it.
2. Search for the tells above and fix each.
3. Check that every claim and number is correct (`fact-check` skill for anything important).
4. Read it aloud mentally: where you would stumble or get bored, fix it.
5. Cut 10-20% length. It almost always gets better.
